# Code of Conduct — ImpactConnect

## Our pledge

ImpactConnect is committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge to act in ways that foster an open, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility and apologizing for mistakes

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hostility based on identity or background
- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Disrupting discussions or events

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the Project Admin via GitHub or the project's communication channel. All reports will be handled confidentially and reviewed promptly. The Project Admin will take appropriate action, which may include warning, temporary suspension, or permanent exclusion from the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — GitHub issues, discussions, pull requests, chat channels, and events related to ImpactConnect.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from common open-source community standards.
